# Privacy policy

KDD for Codex is a skill-only plugin. It does not operate a server, collect
telemetry, transmit prompts, read credentials, or send data to Mauricio Perera
or third parties. Codex and the local tools selected by the user remain subject
to their own policies and settings.

Questions about this policy can be reported through the repository's issue
tracker: https://github.com/MauricioPerera/KDD/issues.
