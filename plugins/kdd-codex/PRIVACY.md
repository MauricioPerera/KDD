# Privacy policy for KDD for Codex

KDD for Codex is a skill published by Mauricio Perera. It gives Codex instructions
for working with software contracts, tests, and verification evidence.

## Using the skill

The skill does not collect personal data or telemetry. It has no server,
account system, or storage operated by the publisher. The publisher does not
receive or retain your prompts, repository files, or test results through
installation or use of the skill.

Codex may process your prompts, selected repository content, and tool output
to perform the work you request. OpenAI handles that processing under its
[Privacy Policy](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/) and your account
or workspace settings. Tools and services you choose to use, such as GitHub
Actions, may process data under their own policies. You can control what you
share with Codex and which project files and tools it can access through your
client and workspace settings. Keep credentials and sensitive project data
out of prompts, contracts, test evidence, and support requests.

## Optional support

If you email [KDD support](https://mauricioperera.github.io/KDD/support.html),
the publisher receives your email address, any name you provide, and the
message and attachments you choose to send. The publisher uses that
information to respond, troubleshoot, and follow up on the request. The email
provider handles delivery and mailbox storage. Support emails are retained
for as long as needed to resolve the request and follow up. There is no fixed
retention period.

If you file a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/MauricioPerera/KDD/issues), your
GitHub username and the information you post are visible to GitHub and the
public. The publisher uses issues to track and resolve reported problems.
Issues can remain in the public record after closure. GitHub applies its own
[privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)
to information it handles. For a private support request, use email and share
only the details needed to reproduce the problem.

## Your choices

You can use the skill without contacting support. Use the support email to
ask about your support data or request access, correction, or deletion.
For a public issue, use GitHub's editing controls and contact the publisher
to request removal of information from the repository's issue tracker.
